# Archived apps

Little Signs has been retired from the public homepage. Its static build and source are preserved in `little-signs/`, accessible at `/archive/little-signs/`.

The original `/little-signs/` URL redirects here to preserve bookmarks. Tiny Signs remains featured on the homepage. This archive does not add or maintain Little Signs features.

Baby Signs is also preserved in `baby-signs/`, accessible at `/archive/baby-signs/`. It is no longer listed on the homepage, and `/baby-signs/` redirects to its archived location.
