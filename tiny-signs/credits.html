Tiny Signs is a personal, noncommercial practice companion. Its hand cues and exercises have not been independently reviewed by an ASL educator. A remembered sign is your self-assessment, not a certified result.

Use these as movement references

ASL Signbank documents variants for research and annotation. Its clips are not narrated lessons or a complete ASL curriculum. They show isolated movements with limited facial expression. Use them to revisit movement, and learn natural expression, variation, and context from qualified Deaf ASL educators. See the creators’ explanation.

Tiny Signs selects one documented variant for each entry. Other valid forms exist. Everyday word prompts differ from the source gloss labels, which identify the precise variants below.
