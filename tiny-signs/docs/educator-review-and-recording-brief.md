# Tiny Signs: educator review and recording brief

Status: prepared for review; no educator has been hired and no independent review has taken place.

## Purpose

Help a parent with no ASL background learn a small set of everyday signs before baby arrives, then model them in natural routines. This is vocabulary practice, not a full ASL course.

## Reviewer / presenter requirements

A qualified Deaf ASL educator with experience teaching adult beginners and familiarity with parent/infant signing. Agree the scope, fee, public-web distribution rights, offline downloads, presenter release, and attribution before commissioning work. No outreach or purchase has been made.

## Review each sign

Check the selected ASL variant, active/supporting hand, handshape, palm direction, body location, movement, repetition, natural facial expression, and the example routine. Rewrite confusing cues. Identify variants explicitly. Confirm that each memory exercise accepts equivalent everyday labels and does not imply that self-assessment certifies accuracy.

## Recording deliverables

For each of 30 signs: a short natural-speed demonstration; a separate slow demonstration; a close-up that keeps fingertips, wrists and contact locations visible; and an oblique/side view where it clarifies palm direction or depth. Include the face in a full-body-context view. Use consistent lighting and framing. Do not digitally mirror clips without explicit orientation labeling. Do not synthesize hand movements.

Record a separate narrated explanation and written transcript describing each hand in plain language. Include a routine example such as a feed, diaper change, or book. Preserve a clean, unlabeled clip for recognition practice. Deliver H.264 MP4 suitable for browser playback and a still image. Final material must allow hosting and device-local offline downloads.

## Current reference library

All clips are unmodified ASL Signbank public reference variants on teal backgrounds. Their CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license permits the current noncommercial reuse with attribution; they are research/annotation references, not narrated teaching content. Full credits are in public/credits.html. No ASL Signbank endorsement or review is claimed.

Source variant: [MILKasym](https://aslsignbank.com/dictionary/gloss/506.html)

Current cues: Curve your fingers and thumb as if holding a small cup. Close your fingers into a fist, then relax them again. Rest your other hand. Only the signing hand opens and closes.

Routine: Right before nursing or offering a bottle, say “milk,” make the sign, pause briefly, then feed your baby.
