A gentle, honest note

These are drawn from American Sign Language, a full language with its own grammar, culture, and regional variation. This page is a friendly starting point for a parent — not a substitute for learning from Deaf educators. Signs vary by region and family, and that is completely okay. What matters most is that you say the word out loud every time you sign it.
